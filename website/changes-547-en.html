# Dune Legacy Tornie 1.0.547

## Visual correction dated 1 November 2026

The corrected **1.0.547** packages centre the Super Tank turret on its chassis in **all eight directions** and at **all three zoom levels**, in game, in the editor and in its previews. Tornie’s original PNG files are preserved.

Download the existing **1.0.547** packages again to receive this correction. The Super Tank costs **925 credits**; network compatibility uses **protocol 25**, and saves use **9840**.

## Kleshmersh Super Tank

The **Super Tank** is a new unit replacing Kleshmersh’s Elite Siege Tank production in Tornie, Jericho and ArcaKev. It costs **925 credits**, has **300 hit points**, matching the standard Siege Tank, and fires a two-projectile incendiary salvo in the style of the Rocket Launcher. Its destruction causes a damaging Devastator-style explosion.

It retains the replaced unit’s access requirements: Heavy Factory and IX, normally available at technology 7, without an additional factory upgrade. Other factions retain their Elite Siege Tank. Kleshmersh scenario starts and reinforcements using that older tank receive a Super Tank when a new mission starts. Tornie’s original turret artwork and dedicated icon are included.

## In-game selection

- **Army**, below the Chemical Carryall button: selects available military units; Harvesters, MCVs and transports retain their own selections.
- **Harvesters**: selects every available variant, including Harvestank, large-capacity and fast Harvesters.
- Counts match selectable units. Allied-control permissions are respected; enemy and unavailable units are excluded. The buttons give way to selected-object controls.

## Editor Windtraps

Across all six non-Vanilla mods, every faction can use the standard 2×2 Windtrap’s **“Pre-upgraded”** editor option **without requiring Chaos Mode**. It doubles initial power, enables the green animation and displays a **green star in the editor**. Undo/redo, map roundtrips and saved games preserve it. This map property requires neither an initial payment nor IX; the purchasable in-game upgrade retains its Rebels and Chaos-blueprint requirements.

## Mission stability

Fixed a crash when closing or reloading a mission while an army remains selected. Co-op checkpoint loading now preserves the save bytes throughout settings restoration.

## Compatibility

All multiplayer participants need **1.0.547 / protocol 25** and the same mod. New saves use **9840**. Earlier saves remain readable with their stored units, technology tables and mission state; start a new game or mission to receive the new replacements. Jericho/ArcaKev campaigns and briefings remain preserved; this release adapts Kleshmersh’s unit without rewriting those campaigns.

The catalogue stays at **73 achievements**. Revised 546 campaigns and co-op, the editor’s Vanilla access and the website’s DuneCity links remain included. See the current follow-up for outstanding human playtests and author feedback.
