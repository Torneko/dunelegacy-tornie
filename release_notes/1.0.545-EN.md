# Dune Legacy Tornie 1.0.545


- Tharpique: healing recharge increased by 50% for Gas Infantry and Chemical
  Siege Tank (1.92 → 2.88 s), and Chemipost (2.4 → 3.6 s). Healing stays at 15 HP;
  enemy deviation attacks and other factions keep their cadence. Chaos follows
  the production blueprint’s faction.
- With nothing selected, Ornithopter and Chemical Carryall shortcut buttons show
  the selectable aircraft count in parentheses, using the player’s faction/bonus
  colour. Elite Ornithopters and allied control are included. These counters
  disappear with their buttons when an object is selected.
- Radar research: common buttons appear at level 4, specialization at level 7;
  the IX remains required to start specialization.
- Westwood signature: approximately +6 dB, starting with the logo’s first animated
  frame after its one-second fade-in. User volume, mute and skip cleanup remain
  respected.
- Network: every participant must download this corrected 1.0.545 (protocol 22).
  Save format remains 9839, with earlier saves still readable.

Radar research, campaigns, faction artwork and 73 achievements. Linux, Linux Mint
and Windows. Every network participant needs this version: protocol 22.
New saves use 9839; previous saves remain readable.

- Right score frame: corrected one-pixel alignment clears the old logo through
  its final inclusive edge. The regression now checks that column explicitly.
- Fremen/Sardaukar Troopers: six separate production icons (1/3/5 soldiers per
  faction) and two dedicated selected-unit portraits, following Troopers.zip
  filenames. All eight PNGs remain unchanged, without added tint or stars.
  WOR, Worfinery and Chaos blueprints use the corresponding production artwork.
- Radar research in every non-Vanilla mod, including Lite: two common upgrades
  (construction range +2 tiles, ground vision +2) and one house specialization.
  Common: 500 credits / 45 seconds / level 4. Specialization: 750 credits /
  60 seconds / level 7 + IX. Powered Radar required; existing/future units,
  saves, deterministic network commands and AI supported. See Radar-Research.md.
- UI refinements: golden frames blend into the actual score/region background,
  without rectangular backing or black fringes; original PNG files retained.
  White Radar price overlays (500 / 500 / 750) are separate from research names
  and progress; labels follow the house colour.
- Main and section menu buttons, including Lite: dark green for Tornie,
  dark grey for Jericho, darker reddish orange for ArcaKev for easier reading.
  These refinements replace the 1.0.545 downloads; download them again.
- Raider Quad: all eight headings anchored to the vanilla Quad at 1×/2×/3×.
- No blue editor star on Slowpost, in the palette or on the map.
- Standard Rebels 2×2 Windtrap: editor pre-upgrade checkbox, immediate double
  power and green animation; map save and undo/redo retain it. Outside Vanilla.
- Chaos: standard 2×2 Windtrap and borrowed Rebels upgrade; Flamepost/Chemipost/
  Slowpost conversions follow borrowed blueprints, including Lite. Older saved
  draws retain their donors.
- Original blue Westwood logo, palette, timing and signature audio. **Replay
  Intro** button above co-op on the right of the main menu.
- Original localized Dune II end credits, eleven illustrations and music.
  Added campaign cutscenes follow CampaignPlan.ini roles, names and colours,
  including Lite and bonus colours.
- Twelve golden faction frames in campaign scores and region selection: correct
  identity across mod and bonus-colour changes, complete borders and unchanged
  original files. Campaign selection/confirmation retain ordinary banners,
  including Neutral’s original colours. This correction replaces the initial
  1.0.545 packages.
- Supplied Fremen/Sardaukar single/three/five production portraits and separate
  selected-unit artwork. No added stars or numbers; Chaos follows the blueprint house.
- Three new achievements and counters, **73 total**: win co-op after elimination
  thanks to a surviving ally; survive and win for an eliminated ally; destroy
  an enemy Palace with a Saboteur. Co-op supports two, three or four allied
  factions; Archon controllers share their house outcome.
- FR/EN website: research, campaigns, portraits, full achievement list, original
  resources and a display crop for Tornie’s supplied Fremen banner.

Existing achievement profiles are retained; the 100% indicator follows all 73.
