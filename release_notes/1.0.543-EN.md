# 1.0.543 — Building sales, alliance rules and new units

Linux, Mint AppImage and Windows share the same source. All participants need **1.0.543 / protocol 17**; new saves use **9835**, older saves remain readable.

## Latest adjustments

- Original three/five flame-Trooper production icons from `troopers_flames.tar.gz` are preserved unchanged in all six mods and follow Kleshmersh technology, including Chaos, without star or number overlays.
- Flame Quad: direct close-range accuracy without long-range rocket scatter or delayed detonation. Fix the one-pixel left bias on odd-width views, including its right-facing view.
- Sales: building destruction effects and sound, then owned concrete across the whole footprint without rubble. The 50% refund, contained-unit evacuation and sale statistics are preserved.
- Sale button after the Upgrade slot, without overlap.
- Jericho/Lite and ArcaKev/Lite: the dark-green bonus colour exactly matches Tornie Rebels; default faction colours are preserved.
- Network protocol 17 prevents mixing the previous projectile/sale behaviour with these changes; version 1.0.543 and save format 9835 are retained.

## Linux display correction

- PulseAudio and XRandR are explicitly compiled and checked before packaging; audio failure details are preserved.
- Linux packages detect physical monitors through XRandR; both AppImages bundle the library.
- Windows retain the selected resolution instead of snapping to the sole desktop mode reported by Linux. Stale dimensions larger than the monitor are fitted to that screen.
- Smaller resolutions remain available in video options even when Linux reports only its native mode. Fullscreen stays on the window’s monitor.
- Replaces 1.0.543 only; save format 9835 is retained.

## Replacement 1.0.543

- Correct Flame Quad and Gas Infantry house tinting across VGA conversion differences; keep the gas weapon green.
- All three new units have blue stars in editor buttons and on editor terrain at 1×/2×/3×. No blue stars are added to portraits; original PNG files remain unchanged.
- Brighter Neutral white in Tornie and ArcaKev.
- Neutral can produce Saboteurs at the Worfinery at level 7 with IX, for 500 credits, in campaign and multiplayer (full Tornie, Jericho and ArcaKev mods). Production is Neutral-only; existing Palace powers are preserved.
- No “5” badge is superimposed on super-squad portraits.
- Other new units, napalm, slowing effects and the tame Sandworm are deferred until artwork and specifications are available.

- Flame Quad: centre each direction on its tile using visible bounds at 1×/2×/3×, in gameplay and the editor. Original PNGs unchanged.
- Exclude Harkonnen flame Troopers from the Special vehicle pool while retaining level 7 + IX production. Gas Infantry is also classified as infantry.
- Network protocol 17: download the replacement on every computer; the handshake rejects earlier 1.0.543 editions using protocols 15 or 16. Save format 9835 retained.

## Locally verified changes

- Sell buildings for 50% of construction price, including damaged buildings, without requiring credit storage. The repair-sized `$` button sits after the Upgrade slot. Enabled by default in Tornie, Tornie Lite, ArcaKev and ArcaKev Lite; configurable in game rules.
- Contained refinery and repair-yard units are evacuated before selling. In-flight Starport deliveries must finish first. Prepaid unfinished production is refunded separately from the building price.
- First sale achievement, plus persistent buildings-sold and sale-credit statistics.
- Non-cooperative multiplayer: independent host permissions for allied control and construction on allied land, restricted to the same team. Ownership and funds remain separate.
- Optional harvester counter, disabled by default in Tornie/Tornie Lite and enabled in other modes.
- Protocol 17 and save format 9835, retaining old save readers.

## New units integrated and locally verified

- Kleshmersh retains default flame Troopers, including borrowed Chaos technology and donor-aware portraits.
- Harkonnen gets a separate flame Trooper product at level 7 requiring IX; ordinary Troopers remain. Outside Vanilla.
- Kleshmersh gets Rocket Trike at level 3/upgrade 1, Flame Quad at level 5/upgrade 2 and no normal Sonic Trike production.
- Ordos and Tharpique Gas Infantry: WOR/Worfinery, IX prerequisite; allied healing and Deviator gas against enemies.
- Five original PNGs preserved in all six mods. Gas sprite magenta is made transparent at load time, opaque black remains, and zoom uses exact 1×/2×/3× scales.
- New units are registered in the editor, reinforcements and save factories using appended IDs.

See the [specification and initial balance](../docs/UNITS-1.0.543.md). The FR/EN website and matching offline archive accompany the Linux/Windows 1.0.543 release.
