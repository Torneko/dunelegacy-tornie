1.0.543 release notes

Linux display fix: XRandR monitor detection, fullscreen on one monitor, selected window size preserved, and smaller resolutions available again in video options. Stale dimensions larger than the monitor are corrected. PulseAudio is explicitly built and checked for Mint startup. Replaces 1.0.543; protocol 18, saves 9836 retained.

Linux, Windows and Mint AppImage

Version 1.0.543: protocol 18, save format 9836. The new units and fixes are integrated and verified in the Linux and Windows packages.

  • Sell buildings for 50% of construction price, without requiring storage; a $ button follows the Upgrade slot. Enabled by default in Tornie, Tornie Lite, ArcaKev and ArcaKev Lite. One achievement and two statistics track sales.
  • Non-cooperative multiplayer: the host independently enables allied control and construction on allied land for houses on the same team.
  • Harvester counter: an option disabled by default in Tornie and Tornie Lite, enabled in other modes.

New units — verified packages

  • Kleshmersh: default flamethrower Troopers outside Vanilla, available through randomized Chaos production technology. Rocket Trike at level 3 / Light Factory upgrade 1; Flame Quad at level 5 / upgrade 2; remove Sonic Trike from normal production.
  • Harkonnen: separate flamethrower Troopers alongside ordinary Troopers, at level 7 with an IX, outside Vanilla.
  • Ordos and Tharpique Gas Infantry: WOR and Worfinery, IX required; heals allies and applies the Deviator effect to enemies.

Local checks passed: engine/network tests, seven modes, 36 sprite renders without magenta backgrounds at 1×/2×/3×, allied healing, IX production gates and saves.

Full notes: units, house colours, stars and Neutral Saboteur.

Latest adjustments: accuracy, three/five icons, editor markers, sale sound and concrete, bonus colours.
